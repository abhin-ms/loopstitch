Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when you can cancel an order and when you receive a refund.

  1. Cancellation: you can cancel an order within 10 days of placing it, as long as it has not been shipped yet. Once an order has been shipped or is out for delivery it can no longer be cancelled, but you may refuse it at the doorstep.
  2. Refunds are issued only (a) when an order is cancelled before it ships, or (b) when a damaged, defective or wrong item cannot be replaced, for example because that design or size has sold out (our drops are never restocked).
  3. We do not offer refunds or exchanges for change of mind, fit or size. Please check the size guide on each product page before ordering.
  4. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 14 days.

Return Policy

We accept returns only for items that arrive damaged, defective (for example a printing or stitching fault) or different from what you ordered (wrong design, colour or size).

  1. Report the problem within 10 days of delivery by emailing hello@loopstitch.online with your order number and photos of the item and its packaging.
  2. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
  3. Return shipping is free: we will arrange the return pickup or reimburse your courier charge.
  4. Once we receive and check the item, we send you a replacement of the same design, colour and size at no cost. If a replacement is not possible, we refund you in full.

We cannot accept returns or exchanges for size or fit, change of mind, or items that have been worn, washed or damaged after delivery. Custom-printed tees are made to your design, so they are replaced only if there is a printing or product fault on our side.

Delivery of all orders will be made to the address provided by the buyer at the time of purchase. Delivery of our services will be confirmed on your email ID as specified at the time of registration/order placement. If there are any shipping cost(s) levied by the seller or the Platform Owner (as the case may be), the same is not refundable.
